Rules

Before the fix, a few pings that ran out before Vesper could answer quietly counted as Vesper saying no. Each one pushed Vesper further down the list, until Vesper's phone stopped ringing altogether, and nothing let Vesper back in.

After the fix, a ping that runs out simply goes to the next person. It doesn't count against Vesper, so Vesper keeps getting asked. Vesper and Aunt Dot can also see what's happening.
